# Launch kit

## For this to work

we must choose:

- one specific customer segment **AND**
- one painful job **AND**
- one clear offer

but we can launch multiple times until we find an angle that consistently results in more clients, where we should change one major variable at a time so you can tell what helped

## Objectives

at launch there are multiple objectives:

- **Customers:** demos, useful problem-solving posts, clear pricing and a trial or paid pilot. Measure purchases and retained customers.
- **Trust:** real product walkthroughs, honest limitations, support/security information and genuine customer evidence.
- **Brand:** a recognizable point of view and consistent examples of the problem you solve.
- **Investors:** market insight, credible traction, retention and a clear fundraising story. Use a separate investor outreach path.
- **SEO and AI discovery:** useful public product, comparison, integration and documentation pages. [Google’s guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)
- **Activation and retention:** onboarding that gets customers to a useful result, then brings them back. This belongs in the kit too.

- **Customers:** Reels bring people to the Instagram profile, and one clear next step (trial, purchase, DM) captures them. Pricing and the trial or paid pilot live on the site. LinkedIn and YouTube bring some inbound and demo requests.
- **Trust:** product walkthroughs on YouTube, pinned on the Instagram profile. Honest limitations, support and security info on the site. Customer evidence on both the profile and the site.
- **Brand:** the point of view is the founder's, on LinkedIn. Instagram repeats consistent examples of the problem.
- **Investors:** not a content channel. They get the separate outreach path (intros, email, deck), and LinkedIn is the public record they check afterwards.
- **SEO and AI discovery:** product, comparison, integration and documentation pages on the site. Demos titled and chaptered on YouTube. Problem-first threads on Reddit.
- **Activation and retention:** happens in the product itself (onboarding, plus email if there is any). Help docs on the site and how-to videos on YouTube support it.

## Activation and retention

this is an **Activation and retention** of the above:

> - **Onboarding:** help them create and send their first invoice
> - **Activation:** they’ve successfully done the useful thing they came for
> - **Retention:** the software keeps helping them manage invoices, so they continue using it

## Repeatable virality in instagram

if repeatable virality in instagram is achivable this

1. **Reels attract attention**

   Make entertaining, surprising or useful content connected to the problem the product solves. The connection matters: people should have a reason to want the product after enjoying the video.

2. **Your profile builds understanding and trust**

   Pinned demonstrations, clear positioning, pricing information, answers to common doubts and genuine customer evidence help interested viewers evaluate you.

3. **One clear next step captures demand**

   Send them to a relevant product page, trial, purchase or requested DM conversation. The destination should continue the promise made in the video.

4. **The product delivers the result**

   Help people complete the first useful task, then measure whether they keep using and paying for it.

### Content roles

That means your kit could be built around three content roles:

- **Reach:** make the right people notice the problem
- **Proof:** show the solution working and answer objections
- **Conversion:** present a specific offer and next step

One video can do all three, but it doesn’t have to. A broadly entertaining Reel can introduce people to a more focused demonstration.
